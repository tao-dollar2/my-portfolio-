The Atelier

“A suit should not announce itself. It should simply make a man look like the best version of himself.”

Basotaj works with a small, deliberate number of commissions so each person receives personal attention, careful drafting and a final fit that feels precise from the first wear.

Our approach combines local tailoring culture with international standards in cloth, construction and finish.

Process

From consultation to final fitting

Every commission follows a measured approach to ensure confidence, comfort and a refined final result.

Consultation

An unhurried conversation covering fit, cloth, occasion and personal preference.

Draft & Cloth

A hand-drafted pattern is refined to your form, with cloth selected to suit your wardrobe.

Fittings

We review balance, shoulder line and drape at every stage to ensure a confident final fit.

Finishing

Hand-finished details, pressing and final presentation with care notes for long-term wear.
