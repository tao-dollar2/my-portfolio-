Our story

Crafted for men who value fit above fashion.

Basotaj was founded on a simple principle: a garment should be made around a man’s form, lifestyle and occasion — never the other way around.

Values

Precision

Every pattern is drawn with intention and adjusted carefully over multiple fittings to achieve a balanced final line.

Material

We work with trusted mills and carefully selected cloths chosen for texture, longevity and seasonal suitability.

Discretion

Our commissions are personal and private, focused on timeless style, measured fit and graceful confidence.

How we work

Measured, personal and considered at every stage.

From consultation to final pressing, each stage is built around communication. We assess posture, preference, occasion and daily wear so the final garment works elegantly in real life.

No garment leaves the atelier without being checked for balance, proportion and finish.
